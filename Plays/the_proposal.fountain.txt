Title: THE PROPOSAL
Credit: Written by
Author: Anton Chekhov
Draft date: 1889
Format: Play

# ACT I

[[SCENE: A room in CHUBUKOV's house.]]

.INT. DRAWING ROOM - DAY

CHUBUKOV enters, wearing a dress-jacket and white gloves. LOMOV enters after him, in a dress-suit and white gloves.

CHUBUKOV
(going to meet him)
My dear fellow, whom do I see? Ivan Vassilevitch! I am extremely glad!

He squeezes his hand.

CHUBUKOV (CONT'D)
Now this is a surprise, my boy... How are you?

LOMOV
Thank you. And how may you be getting on?

CHUBUKOV
We just get along somehow, my angel, thanks to your prayers, and so on. Sit down, please do... Now, you know, you shouldn't forget your neighbors, my darling. But why are you so formal in your get-up? Evening dress, gloves, and so on. Are you going anywhere, my treasure?

LOMOV
No, I've come only to see you, honored Stepan Stepanovitch.

CHUBUKOV
Then why are you in evening dress, my precious? As if you're paying a New Year's Eve visit!

LOMOV
Well, you see, it's like this. 
(takes his arm)
